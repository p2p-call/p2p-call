⚙ Settings
Preferred tunnel

The tunnel gives the call an address on the internet; the conversation doesn't go through it. localhost.run has no extra pages but is slow: with a direct link, the page takes about ten seconds to open for your friend. serveo is fast, but with a direct link your friend first has to click "Continue to Site". Cloudflare downloads its own program (~40 MB) and doesn't work on every network.

My permanent link
